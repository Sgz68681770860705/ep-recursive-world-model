## What is established

1. A reversible, energy-conserving total model can produce an apparently dissipative memory term after background variables are eliminated.
2. A causal kernel defines a generalized viscosity \(\widehat\eta(k,\omega)\).
3. A one-pole response combines viscosity, time scale, and spatial scale in one formula.
4. Within that model class, the slow-mode parameters are identifiable from sufficiently rich complex response data.

## What remains open

- derive \(J_k(\Omega)\) from explicit finite-\(N\) EP collision dynamics;
- derive tensor anisotropy from quaternion orientation and deformable \(S_0\) boundaries;
- prove the kinetic and hydrodynamic limits;
- recover Navier–Stokes with coefficients calculated from the microscopic model;
- test whether any measured real-fluid response requires the proposed EP variables.
