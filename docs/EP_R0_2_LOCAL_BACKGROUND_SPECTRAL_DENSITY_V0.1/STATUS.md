## Established in this model

1. A local finite-size source and a local background field determine the bath spectral density.
2. In three dimensions, a gapless linear background with effective non-derivative coupling is Ohmic.
3. The ordinary viscosity, memory time, and spatial response scale are:
   \[
   \eta_0=\frac{2\sqrt{\pi}g^2a^3}{c_b^3},
   \qquad
   \tau_{\rm mem}\sim\frac{a}{c_b},
   \qquad
   \eta(k,0)=\eta_0e^{-a^2k^2}.
   \]
4. Derivative order and spatial dimension determine whether the channel is sub-Ohmic, Ohmic, or super-Ohmic.
5. A gapped channel cannot provide nonzero DC viscosity.
