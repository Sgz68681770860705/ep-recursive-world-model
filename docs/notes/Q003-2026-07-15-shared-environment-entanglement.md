## 1. Correction to an earlier simplification

Entanglement must not be reduced to only:

\[
(+1,-1)\quad\text{or}\quad(-1,+1).
\]

The full joint output space contains:

\[
(+1,+1),\quad(-1,-1),\quad(+1,-1),\quad(-1,+1).
\]

The central issue is not mandatory opposition. It is a joint constraint that may support synchronization, anti-synchronization, or a general phase relation.

## 2. Central conjecture

> **[CONJECTURE] \(S_0\) objects are observable energy quanta, while \(S_{-1}\) is the deeper environment that maintains and connects them. When an entangled pair is created, the two visible \(S_0\) endpoints and a shared \(S_{-1}\) structure form one definite joint phase state. The complete underlying state may be determinate before measurement, while an \(S_0\)-level observer has access only to a probabilistic projection.**

Define:

\[
\Lambda_{AB}=
(S_0^A,S_0^B,\Xi_{-1}^{AB}),
\]

where \(\Xi_{-1}^{AB}\) is a shared environment, not merely two independent local backgrounds.

## 3. A synchronization ledger

Introduce a candidate relative phase:

\[
\delta=\phi_B-\phi_A.
\]

- \(\delta=0\): synchronized correlation.
- \(\delta=\pi\): anti-synchronized correlation.
- General \(\delta\): intermediate phase relation.

The initialization may depend on position, background, preparation history, and boundary state:

\[
\delta=\delta(\mathbf r_A,\mathbf r_B,X_{-1},\text{history}).
\]

The “shared ledger” idea means that the correlation is established at pair creation and persists as long as the joint environment remains coherent.

## 4. Measurement as collision, coupling, and manifestation

A detector is not a passive eye. It is an \(S_0\)-and-above physical structure that interacts with the measured object.

Candidate EP measurement chain:

\[
\text{joint underlying state}
+
\text{apparatus state}
+
\text{measurement direction}
\rightarrow
\text{collision/boundary reconstruction}
\rightarrow
\text{discrete record}.
\]

Thus the model may retain both claims:

1. The complete state was definite before measurement.
2. Measurement is still a real physical interaction.

A careful “turning over a card” statement is therefore: measurement need not create reality from nothing; it makes a pre-existing joint state manifest under a concrete apparatus and direction.

## 5. Measurement order is not the primary variable

If the shared environment exists from pair creation and remains coherent, the ordering of distant measurements need not generate the correlation. More important are the integrity of the joint environment, apparatus coupling, measurement directions, and environmental noise.

## 6. Multi-direction statistics must be derived

Allowing both synchronization and anti-synchronization corrects a one-sided picture, but does not by itself derive arbitrary-angle correlations.

The model must calculate:

\[
P_{++}(a,b),\quad
P_{+-}(a,b),\quad
P_{-+}(a,b),\quad
P_{--}(a,b),
\]

and:

\[
E(a,b)=P_{++}+P_{--}-P_{+-}-P_{-+}.
\]

A candidate shared-geometry structure is:

\[
\mathbf q_B=\mathbf R_{AB}\mathbf q_A,
\]

with outputs generated by the full collision response:

\[
(A,B)=\mathcal M_{\mathbf a,\mathbf b}
[\Lambda_{AB},D_A,D_B].
\]

The target angular function must not be inserted by hand.

## 7. Relation to existing experiments

The conjecture does not logically have to contradict entanglement experiments, but compatibility must be demonstrated.

Experiments may not directly observe:

\[
\rho_{-1},\quad u_{-1},\quad\Xi_{-1}^{AB},
\]

but they observe the induced \(S_0\)-level map:

\[
S_{-1}\text{ mechanism}\longrightarrow P(A,B|a,b).
\]

Bell-type results do not exclude every deterministic ontology. They constrain models that combine local factorization, independent settings, and fixed local response rules. EP must state explicitly whether it uses a nonlocal joint constraint, contextual deterministic dynamics, fast deep reconstruction, or a stronger global initial-condition structure.

## 8. Thermal motion and decoherence

Thermal and environmental collisions may perturb the shared phase ledger.

A minimal phase-diffusion model is:

\[
d\delta=\sqrt{2D_T}\,dW_t,
\]

with coherence measure:

\[
C_{AB}(t)=
\left|\left\langle e^{i\delta(t)}\right\rangle\right|.
\]

In the simplest case:

\[
C_{AB}(t)=e^{-D_Tt}.
\]

Decoherence is then not a card changing arbitrarily; it is the spreading of an initially concentrated A–B correlation into many environmental degrees of freedom.

## 9. Fast \(S_{-1}\) response

The conjecture allows:

\[
u_{-1}\gg c_\gamma.
\]

At laboratory distances, \(L/u_{-1}\) may appear instantaneous to every \(S_0\)-level instrument.

This does not automatically prove controllable faster-than-light signaling, derive the angular statistics, or remove all indirect experimental constraints. The internal speed may be unobservable directly, while its output map remains testable.
